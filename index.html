1. Introduction

Welcome to GOODTIME. Your privacy is important to us. GOODTIME is an Android application that provides alarm, world clock, stopwatch and countdown timer features. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used and how it is stored.

This policy must accurately reflect the app version you publish. The statements below assume that the released app operates without account registration, developer-operated servers, advertising or analytics services.

2. Information We Collect

GOODTIME does not ask you to create an account or provide information such as your name, email address, phone number or home address to use its core features. The app is not designed to transmit personal information to a developer-operated server.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

GOODTIME may store app settings locally on your Android device to provide its features. This may include:

  • Alarm times, schedules, labels and enabled/disabled status.
  • Ringtone and vibration preferences.
  • Selected World Clock cities.
  • Timer and stopwatch preferences or state, where supported.
  • Application theme and display preferences.

This information is used to operate and personalize the app. It is not intentionally sent to the developer's servers.

4. Android Permissions

Depending on your Android version and enabled features, GOODTIME may use the following permissions or system capabilities:

  • Notifications: to show alarm and timer notifications.
  • Exact alarms: to schedule alarms at the times you choose, where Android requires special access.
  • Vibration: to vibrate the device when an alarm rings, if enabled.
  • Boot completed: to restore enabled alarms after the device restarts, where supported.
  • Full-screen intent: to display the alarm screen when an alarm rings, subject to Android rules and user settings.
  • Foreground service and audio-related permissions: to support alarm playback and related background operation.

Permissions are used for the app's functionality. Availability and behavior may vary by Android version and device manufacturer.
